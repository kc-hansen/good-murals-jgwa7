# Good Murals — Customer Research Summary

This document describes the business, what it offers, and who it serves — written to share with the artist for review and approval before build begins.

---

## What Good Murals Does

Good Murals creates custom, hand-painted murals for residential interiors. Every mural is an original — designed for a specific wall in a specific room, painted by hand, and permanently installed.

Two distinct styles:

**Abstract** — Gestural, color-driven work at room scale. Bold sweeps of color, organic forms, and the unmistakable texture of a hand on a wall. These are murals that fill a room with feeling and energy.

**Tattoo / Illustrative** — Precise, narrative, intricate. Botanical forms, custom imagery, line work drawn with the same care and detail as fine tattoo art. These are murals that reward close inspection.

---

## Who This Is For

Homeowners who want something on their walls that no one else has. People who've invested in their home and want the walls to match that investment. People who are tired of art that doesn't quite fit, or walls that don't do anything.

Also: interior designers sourcing a custom feature for a client's renovation — someone who needs a specialist they can trust to show up, communicate, deliver, and make the room irreplaceable.

The price range ($500–$1,500) makes custom, hand-painted mural work accessible to a wider range of homeowners than the high-end art market typically reaches — without compromising the quality or the craft.

---

## How a Commission Works

1. **Tell us about your wall** — dimensions, room type, style preference, timeline. Submit with a $250 qualifying deposit (credited to your project total).
2. **We talk** — within 48 hours, we follow up to discuss your vision in more detail, confirm the scope, and agree on a price.
3. **You see it before we paint it** — we provide a digital mockup of the design in your actual space. We refine until you're completely happy.
4. **We come to you** — we travel to client locations and paint on-site. Most projects take 2–5 days.
5. **The room is transformed** — permanently, irreplaceably, by hand.

## What the Website Does

The site exists to do one thing: make it easy for the right client to submit a commission request.

The portfolio shows the work — large, gallery-quality images of completed murals in real homes. The commission page makes the process clear and the first step concrete: tell us about your wall, pay a small qualifying deposit, and we'll be in touch within 48 hours.

Everything on the site is the artist's real work. No stock photos, no generic imagery.

## What Good Murals Is NOT

- A mural printing service (these are hand-painted, not printed)
- A removable or temporary wall treatment (these are permanent)
- A budget decorator (the price reflects skill, custom design, and on-site execution)
- A commercial muralist taking on retail or restaurant projects (this is residential, private spaces only)
